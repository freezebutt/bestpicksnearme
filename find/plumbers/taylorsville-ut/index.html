Common questions

Do plumbers in Utah need a license?

Yes. Plumbers in Utah are licensed by the Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL). You can look up a plumber's license status on the DOPL website before you hire.

What should I ask a plumber before booking?

Ask whether they are licensed and insured, whether there is a call-out or trip fee, how they price the job (flat rate or hourly), and whether the work and parts carry a warranty.

When do I need an emergency plumber?

Call one for a burst pipe, a sewer backup, no water at all, a gas smell near a water heater, or a leak you cannot shut off at the fixture or the main valve.

Should I repair or replace an old water heater?

Tank water heaters usually last about 8 to 12 years. If yours is in that range and leaking from the tank, replacement is usually the better value. A plumber can tell you after an inspection.
