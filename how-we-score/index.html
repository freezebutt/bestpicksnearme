How we rank

Every business is checked the same way, and nobody can pay to rank higher. Featured listings pay for placement.

Featured listings (Diamond, then Gold, then Silver) pay for placement and come first on the city pages they choose. Then come Claimed listings: non-paid businesses whose owner has confirmed the listing with us. See packages.

For non-paid listings, businesses based in or serving that city come first, then nearby ones. Within each group, the order comes from these checks, most important first:

  1. Reviews. The business's average rating and number of public reviews, checked weekly. A handful of reviews counts for less than hundreds, so a 5.0 from 3 reviews does not beat a 4.8 from 500.
  2. Licence. A current licence with Utah's Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL). Coming soon.
  3. Years in business, from the business's own website.
  4. A complete listing: phone, website, street address, a description and services or areas.
  5. Responds when we call or email to confirm the listing. Coming soon.
  6. Local: based in Salt Lake County with a street address.
