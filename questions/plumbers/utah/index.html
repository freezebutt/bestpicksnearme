Hiring a plumber in Utah: questions answered

Straight answers from official sources. Each answer links the page it comes from. Checked 26 Nov 2026.

Do plumbers in Utah need a license?

Yes. Plumbers in Utah are licensed by the Division of Professional Licensing (DOPL), which issues journeyman and master plumber licenses.

Source: Utah DOPL, Plumbing, checked 26 Nov 2026.

How do I check a Utah plumber's license?

Use the "Verify a License" service linked from DOPL's website. You can also call DOPL on (801) 530-6628.

Source: Utah DOPL, Plumbing, checked 26 Nov 2026.

What does it take to become a journeyman plumber in Utah?

DOPL requires a plumber's apprenticeship education program (576 classroom hours) plus 8,000 hours of licensed plumbing experience under a licensed journeyman or master plumber, or 16,000 hours of licensed plumber experience under supervision.

Source: Utah DOPL, Apply for a journeyman plumber license, checked 26 Nov 2026.

Do I need a permit for plumbing work in Salt Lake City?

Yes. Salt Lake City requires a permit to install or alter plumbing systems. If the work counts as general maintenance or repair under $3,000 in value, the owner or their representative may get the permit. Other cities in Salt Lake County set their own rules, so check with your city's building department.

Source: Salt Lake City Building Services, FAQs, checked 26 Nov 2026.
