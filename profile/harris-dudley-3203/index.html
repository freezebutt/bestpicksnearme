Harris-Dudley

Harris-Dudley Co. is a family-owned company that provides heating and air, radiant heating, snow melt systems and plumbing services in Salt Lake City, Park City and surrounding communities. The company began in 1931, with roots going back to 1909. It specializes in hydronic radiant floor heating and runs a radiant-heated showroom at its Salt Lake City headquarters.
